# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first version of the OpenPilates app, for iPhone and iPad. It carries over what
[OpenPilates Server](https://github.com/d-plenty/OpenPilates-server) 0.6.4 does, rebuilt to run
entirely on the device.

**New in the app**

- Everything is kept on the device. No account, no sign-in, no server.
- **My studios**: more than one studio, each with its reformer brand, spring counts, props and the
  class types taught there. One is your usual studio. On Play the app asks which studio you are at
  (when you have more than one), shows that studio's springs and says if a prop is missing there.
- **Calendar**: a date and time on a class, a month view, and a log of classes taught, filled in
  when a class is finished in Play.
- **Share by QR code** to another device on the same Wi-Fi (sealed in transit, one use, ten minutes),
  or as a file through the share sheet.
- **Backups** about once a day when the app opens, kept in Files → On My iPhone → OpenPilates.
- **Look and colours**: light, dark or match the device; seven colours or your own; true black;
  three text sizes.
- An iPad layout with the tabs down the side.

**Carried over**

- The library (184 movements, 18 series) with drawn figures; your own movements and series; hiding.
- The block builder with optional phases, timed lines, layers, finishers, options, undo and redo.
- Drafting a class, suggestions, the checks, the run sheet, Play.
- Class set-up: class types, levels, focuses, timing, parts of the class, your own lists, balance targets.
- Reading OpenPilates Server's flow files and studio packs.

**Not in this version**

- Clients and their health notes. They return with the studio server.
- Sign-in, instructors and roles: the app is one instructor on one device.
- Sync. Shown in the app as coming soon, with the studio server, a computer version, widgets and
  Apple Watch control.
