# Changelog

## 0.2.0

From the first week on Sarah's iPhone.

**Fixed**

- The app fits the screen from the first open: it no longer slides sideways or up and down, and
  nothing sits under the clock.
- Tapping a text box no longer zooms the screen in.
- Springs follow the studio's reformer everywhere: the library, movement pages, the builder,
  blocks, drafts, spring changes and Play. A block shows the spring the class actually uses, and
  says when that differs from the movement's usual (it keeps the setting from the block before, to
  save a change).

**Everything belongs to a studio**

- Each studio has its own class set-up: class types, levels, focuses, lengths and timing, parts of
  the class, prop and muscle names, and what a balanced class means. Props you own have counts.
- A new studio starts blank. The app asks for what it needs as you go: set up a studio when you
  first open it (or skip), and your first class at a studio walks you through its first class type.
- Your movement library, edits and series stay yours, the same at every studio.
- Updating from 0.1.0 copies the old shared set-up into each studio, so nothing is lost.

**Save, like Restock**

- Nothing is saved until you tap Save, top right: classes, studios, class set-up, settings and the
  movement editor. Back asks whether to save or leave your changes.
- The class builder keeps undo and redo beside Save. Play, Share and Duplicate ask you to save first.
- Colours and dark mode show straight away and go back if you leave without saving.

**Classes**

- A new class asks which studio it's for. "Built for" at the top of a class shows the studio, and
  Change moves the class to another studio (springs convert, missing props are flagged).
- Delete (or throw away a new class) is a button at the top of the class.
- Props per block, including props the movement doesn't list. Your own props can be added to the
  studio too. A props bar at the top of the class shows every prop used and how many blocks use it.
- Easier and harder options on a block: progressions as well as regressions, and your own of either.
- Filters when adding a block: part of the class, position, spine and muscle.

**Also**

- Your own notes on any movement.
- The movement editor has everything from OpenPilates Server, in sections: springs to progress,
  headrest and straps, spring note, effort, plane, patterns, movement categories, what stays still
  and what moves, build up to it with, take care with, pregnancy and labels.
- Make a pack: a studio's set-up, your movements and series, and classes, readable by OpenPilates
  Server and by the app.
- Settings → Accessibility, as in Restock: text size, bold text, high contrast, easier reading
  spacing, underlined links, bigger buttons, less motion and messages that stay longer.
- True black in dark mode is on by default.
- The guide links to each part of the app.

## 0.1.0

The first version of the OpenPilates app, for iPhone and iPad. It carries over what
[OpenPilates Server](https://github.com/d-plenty/OpenPilates-server) 0.6.4 does, rebuilt to run
entirely on the device.

**New in the app**

- Everything is kept on the device. No account, no sign-in, no server.
- **My studios**: more than one studio, each with its reformer brand, spring counts, props and the
  class types taught there. One is your usual studio. On Play the app asks which studio you are at
  (when you have more than one), shows that studio's springs and says if a prop is missing there.
- **Calendar**: a date and time on a class, a month view, and a log of classes taught, filled in
  when a class is finished in Play.
- **Share by QR code** to another device on the same Wi-Fi (sealed in transit, one use, ten minutes),
  or as a file through the share sheet.
- **Backups** about once a day when the app opens, kept in Files → On My iPhone → OpenPilates.
- **Look and colours**: light, dark or match the device; seven colours or your own; true black;
  three text sizes.
- An iPad layout with the tabs down the side.

**Carried over**

- The library (184 movements, 18 series) with drawn figures; your own movements and series; hiding.
- The block builder with optional phases, timed lines, layers, finishers, options, undo and redo.
- Drafting a class, suggestions, the checks, the run sheet, Play.
- Class set-up: class types, levels, focuses, timing, parts of the class, your own lists, balance targets.
- Reading OpenPilates Server's flow files and studio packs.

**Not in this version**

- Clients and their health notes. They return with the studio server.
- Sign-in, instructors and roles: the app is one instructor on one device.
- Sync. Shown in the app as coming soon, with the studio server, a computer version, widgets and
  Apple Watch control.
